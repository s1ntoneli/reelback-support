1. What Reelback does

Reelback shows the videos and Live Photos you authorize from Apple Photos as a swipeable feed and lets you set items aside in Reelback's Trash. Reelback works on your device and does not host your media.

2. Deleting media

Moving an item to Reelback's Trash does not delete it. Media is deleted only when you empty the Trash and confirm the iOS system prompt. Deleted items go to Photos › Recently Deleted and can be recovered there for 30 days, after which Apple removes them permanently. If iCloud Photos is on, deletions apply to all devices using that library. You are responsible for reviewing what you delete and for keeping backups of media you want to keep.

3. Reelback Lifetime

Swiping, Smart Cuts, and moving items to Trash are free. Emptying the Trash is free for your first 20 items; after that, emptying requires Reelback Lifetime, a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It is not a subscription and does not renew. Reelback Lifetime is tied to your Apple Account, can be restored with "Restore Purchases", and can be shared through Apple Family Sharing. The price is shown in the app before you buy. Payments, refunds, and billing are handled by Apple under the App Store's terms; refund requests go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
